Thang D. Chu

M.Sc. student in Computing Science at University of Alberta

I am an M.Sc. student in Computing Science at the University of Alberta, advised by Prof. Csaba Szepesvári. I expect to complete the degree in August 2026. My thesis is Lower Bound on Policy Gradient with Decreasing Stepsizes, with a focus on lower bounds for policy-gradient methods that use decreasing stepsizes.

My research interests are reinforcement learning theory, stochastic optimization, continual learning, and LLM post-training. More broadly, I am interested in rigorously understanding when learning algorithms work, when they fail, and how their optimization dynamics shape their statistical performance.

Background

I completed a B.Sc. in Computing Science at the University of Alberta in 2023. My research experience includes:

  • Bilevel Soft Actor-Critic (Carleton University, with Prof. Junfeng Wen): designed a bilevel training strategy and established asymptotic and non-asymptotic convergence guarantees for the resulting method.
  • Kernel representations of Gaussian processes (University of Alberta, with Prof. Martha White): characterized when Gaussian processes and Bayesian linear regression induce equivalent kernel representations across parameterizations.
  • Graph Transformer for drug response prediction (National Economics University, with Nguyen Thanh Tuan): developed GraTransDRP using graph-transformer drug representations, multi-omics encoders, and KernelPCA, achieving a Pearson correlation of (r=0.93) on drug-response benchmarks.

I also worked on large-vessel-occlusion detection from EEG, developing EEG- and clinical-data classifiers that achieved 90% accuracy.

Recognition

I received the Dean’s Silver Medal in Science in 2023 and was named to the Dean’s Honor Roll from 2019 to 2022. I also received the International Student Scholarship, Faculty of Science Gold Standard Scholarship, and University of Alberta Maple Leaf First Year Excellence Scholarship.

Technical skills

I work primarily in Python, Julia, C++, SQL, MATLAB, and R, with machine-learning experience in JAX and PyTorch.
